# 59 Full-Stack Project Ideas

## 1. E-Commerce Platform

This is an online shopping website where users can create accounts and buy products.

Customers can browse products, search and filter them, add products to their cart and wishlist, and make payments. They can also track orders, download invoices, and give reviews.

Sellers can add products and manage their stock. Admins can manage users, products, orders, and view sales information.

---

## 2. Online Food Delivery System

This project is a food delivery website where users can find restaurants and order food.

Users can search restaurants by food type and rating, select food items, add them to the cart, and place orders. They can see the order status from preparation to delivery.

Restaurants can manage their menus and incoming orders. Delivery partners can see delivery information. Users can also rate restaurants and delivery partners.

---

## 3. Inventory Management System

This system helps businesses keep track of their products and stock.

It can manage stock in different warehouses and show alerts when products are running low. Staff can add suppliers, create purchase orders, and record stock movements.

Managers can also view reports about stock usage, old products, and inventory levels.

---

## 4. Learning Management System

This is an online learning platform where teachers can create courses and students can learn through them.

Teachers can upload videos, PDFs, assignments, and quizzes. Students can submit assignments, take quizzes, and track their course progress.

After completing a course, students can receive a certificate. Teachers can also see student activity and performance.

---

## 5. Social Media Platform

This project is a simple social media application where users can create profiles and share content.

Users can post images and videos, like and comment on posts, follow other users, and send private messages.

The application can also have stories that disappear after 24 hours, event creation, friend suggestions, and a system for reporting inappropriate content.

---

## 6. Healthcare Appointment System

This website helps patients find doctors and book appointments.

Patients can search doctors by specialty and availability. They can book, cancel, or reschedule appointments.

Doctors can manage their schedules and patient information. The system can also provide appointment reminders and links for online consultations.

---

## 7. Employee Management System

This project is designed to help companies manage their employees.

Admins can add employees, assign departments and roles, and manage attendance and leave requests.

Employees can view their information, attendance, leave details, and payslips. Managers can also conduct performance reviews.

---

## 8. Video Streaming Platform

This is a video streaming website where users can browse and watch videos.

Users can search videos by category, create watchlists, and continue watching videos from where they stopped.

Creators can upload videos and manage their content. The system can also include subscriptions, parental controls, and video recommendations.

---

## 9. Expense Tracker

This application helps users keep track of their daily expenses.

Users can add income and expenses and organize them into categories. They can set budgets and receive alerts when they spend too much.

The application can show charts for monthly spending and allow users to export their financial information.

---

## 10. Online Examination System

This is an online exam platform for students and teachers.

Teachers can create question papers using multiple-choice, essay, and coding questions. Questions can be shown in random order for different students.

The system includes a timer and automatically submits the exam when time is over. Objective questions can be graded automatically.

Teachers can later check essay answers and view student performance.

---

## 11. Car Rental System

This website allows users to search and rent cars.

Users can select a car based on location, date, and price. They can make a booking and receive a digital rental agreement.

Admins can manage cars, maintenance schedules, and rental information. Users can also upload photos of vehicle damage when returning a car.

---

## 12. Weather App

This application shows current weather information and forecasts.

Users can see the current temperature, weather conditions, hourly information, and a seven-day forecast.

They can save different locations and change between Celsius and Fahrenheit. The app can also provide weather alerts and simple suggestions such as carrying an umbrella.

---

## 13. Online Voting System

This project is a secure online voting system.

Users can register and verify their identity before voting. They can view candidates or proposals and cast their vote only once.

The system keeps an audit record and allows administrators to schedule elections. After the election closes, the results can be displayed using charts.

---

## 14. Fitness Tracker

This application helps users keep track of their fitness activities.

Users can record workouts, steps, calories, sleep, and other fitness information. They can also create personal goals.

The app can show progress through charts and provide achievements and streaks based on the user's activity.

---

## 15. File Sharing Platform

This is a website where users can upload and share files.

Users can drag and drop files, create folders, and share files with other people. They can decide whether others can view or edit a file.

The system can also keep different versions of files and show storage usage.

---

## 16. Content Management System

This project allows users to create and manage website content.

Users can create pages using different content blocks and templates. Pages can go through different stages such as draft, review, and publish.

The system also includes SEO information, version history, and the ability to restore older versions.

---

## 17. Online Banking System

This is a simulated online banking application.

Users can view account balances, transfer money, pay bills, and check transaction history.

They can also manage multiple accounts, create recurring payments, request loans, and download bank statements.

---

## 18. Event Management System

This website helps organizers create and manage events.

Organizers can create event pages, sell different types of tickets, manage seating, and check registrations.

Attendees can register and receive reminders. QR codes can be used for event check-in. Organizers can also view attendance and revenue reports.

---

## 19. Health Records System

This system is designed to manage patient health records.

Patients can view their medical history, prescriptions, and laboratory reports. Doctors can update patient records when necessary.

Every time a record is accessed, the system can keep an access log. Patients can also download their records.

---

## 20. Travel Booking Platform

This website helps users plan and book trips.

Users can search for flights, hotels, and transportation. They can compare prices and create a complete travel itinerary.

The system can also save previous trips and provide notifications about price changes and travel alerts.

---

## 21. Learning Hub

This is an online platform for learning resources.

Students can find articles, videos, PDFs, and quizzes. They can track their progress and maintain learning streaks.

Teachers can upload learning materials and assignments. Students can also receive certificates after completing learning programs.

---

## 22. Hospital Management System

This project helps hospitals manage their daily activities.

Staff can manage patient admissions, appointments, prescriptions, beds, and wards.

The system can also manage staff schedules and billing. Hospital administrators can view information about occupancy and available resources.

---

## 23. Online Art Gallery

This is an online gallery where artists can display and sell their artwork.

Artists can create profiles and upload artwork with descriptions and prices. Customers can browse and purchase artwork.

The platform can also support live auctions where users can place bids. Customers can leave reviews and explore different exhibitions.

---

## 24. CRM System

A CRM system helps companies manage customers and sales.

Employees can store customer information, track leads, and manage sales through a pipeline.

The system can record customer interactions, create follow-up reminders, and show sales reports and forecasts.

---

## 25. News Aggregator

This website collects news from different sources in one place.

Users can browse news by categories such as technology, sports, politics, and more.

They can search for articles, save articles for later, and receive recommendations based on their reading history.

---

## 26. Expense Sharing App

This application helps groups share expenses.

Users can create groups and add expenses. Expenses can be divided equally or by percentage or exact amounts.

The system calculates who owes money to whom and helps simplify the payments between group members.

---

## 27. Food Recipe App

This is a recipe-sharing application.

Users can upload recipes with ingredients and step-by-step instructions. Other users can search recipes based on ingredients and dietary preferences.

Users can save their favorite recipes, leave ratings and comments, and use a special cooking mode while preparing food.

---

## 28. Property Management System

This system helps property owners manage their properties and tenants.

Owners can add properties, manage tenant applications, create lease documents, and track rent payments.

Tenants can submit maintenance requests. Owners can view information about rent, occupancy, and property income.

---

## 29. Online Auction System

This is a real-time online auction website.

Sellers can upload items and set prices and auction times. Buyers can place bids while the auction is running.

Users can receive notifications when someone outbids them. The system can also handle automatic bidding and payment after the auction ends.

---

## 30. Personal Portfolio Website

This website is used to show a person's skills, projects, and experience.

It can include a project gallery, case studies, skills, education, experience, and a blog.

The website can also have a contact form and a downloadable resume. It should have a simple and professional design.

---

## 31. Ride-Sharing App

This application connects passengers with drivers.

Passengers can request rides and see an estimated fare. Drivers can accept rides and navigate to the passenger.

Users can see the driver's location on a map and communicate through chat. After the ride, both sides can give ratings.

---

## 32. Online Grocery Store

This is an online store for buying groceries.

Users can browse products, add quantities to their cart, choose delivery times, and place orders.

The system can also handle unavailable products by allowing customers to choose substitutions. Admins can manage stock and track orders.

---

## 33. Music Streaming App

This is a music streaming application where users can listen to songs.

Users can browse music by artist, album, and genre. They can create playlists, manage their queue, and save songs.

The application can also show lyrics and recommend songs based on listening history.

---

## 34. Forum Application

This project is an online discussion forum.

Users can create topics, reply to discussions, and vote on posts. Discussions can be organized into different categories.

Moderators can manage posts and users. The platform can also have reputation points, badges, notifications, and search.

---

## 35. Online Ticket Booking

This system allows users to book tickets for movies or events.

Users can view available shows and select seats from an interactive seat map.

The system prevents two people from booking the same seat at the same time. After payment, users receive a digital ticket with a QR code.

---

## 36. Student Report System

This system helps schools and colleges manage student marks and attendance.

Teachers can enter marks and attendance. The system can automatically create student reports.

Students and parents can log in to view results and attendance. Teachers can also see performance trends across different semesters.

---

## 37. Task Management System

This is a project and task management application.

Users can create projects, add tasks, assign tasks to team members, and set deadlines.

Tasks can be moved between different stages using a Kanban board. The system can also include subtasks, reminders, and activity history.

---

## 38. Quiz Builder

This application allows users to create and share quizzes.

Quiz creators can add multiple-choice, true-or-false, and open-ended questions.

Users can share quizzes through links. The system can automatically calculate scores and show a live leaderboard for quizzes happening at the same time.

---

## 39. Food Waste Management System

This platform helps reduce food waste by connecting people with extra food to people or organizations that need it.

Donors can post available food and provide its expiry time and location.

Nearby users or charities can claim the food and arrange pickup. The system can also show how much food has been saved and the environmental impact.

---

## 40. Pet Adoption Platform

This website helps people find pets available for adoption.

Shelters can create pet profiles with photos, breed information, and health records.

People interested in adopting can submit applications and communicate with shelters. They can also save pets to their favorites.

---

## 41. Online Donation Platform

This platform allows people to create and support donation campaigns.

Campaign creators can set fundraising goals and post updates. Donors can make online payments and see their donation history.

The system can also generate donation receipts and show how collected funds are being used.

---

## 42. Disaster Management System

This system helps manage emergency and disaster situations.

Users can report incidents and mark their locations on a map. Volunteers and resources can then be assigned to areas that need help.

The system can track requests and resources and send alerts to people in affected areas.

---

## 43. Budget Planner

This application helps users manage their personal budgets.

Users can add their income and expenses and set spending limits for different categories.

They can create savings goals and track their progress. Charts and monthly reports help users understand their spending habits.

---

## 44. Blood Banking System

This system helps blood banks manage donors and blood supplies.

Donors can register their blood group and donation history. The system can show current blood stock for different blood groups.

Hospitals can send blood requests and the system can find suitable donors when needed. Low-stock alerts can also be provided.

---

## 45. Agriculture Project

The system can also show market price trends, weather information, farming advice, and sales information for farmers.

## 46. Hotel Booking Platform

This website allows users to search and book hotel rooms.

Users can filter hotels based on location, price, dates, and facilities. They can check room availability and make bookings.

## 47. Online Library Management System

This system helps libraries manage books and users.

Users can search for books and see whether they are available. They can reserve and borrow books.

The system can track due dates and fines. Librarians can add or remove books and generate reports about borrowed and overdue books.

## 48. Feedback Management System

This platform helps businesses collect and manage customer feedback.

Admins can create feedback forms and collect ratings and written responses.

Feedback can be organized into categories and analyzed to find common problems and trends. Businesses can also respond to customers and send follow-up messages.

---

## 49. Home Tutor Finder

Students can search for tutors based on subject, location, availability, qualifications, and reviews.

Tutors can create profiles, set their fees, manage schedules, and communicate with students. Students can book classes and provide ratings.

---

## 50. Music Streaming Platform

Users can explore music by genre, follow artists, search songs and albums, and create playlists.

---

## 51. Freelancer Marketplace

This platform connects freelancers with people looking for services.

Clients can post jobs and freelancers can submit proposals. Freelancers can create profiles showing their skills and previous work.

---

## 52. Online Job Application Tracker

This application helps people keep track of their job applications.

Users can save jobs and organize applications into stages such as applied, interview, offer, and rejected.

---

## 53. Research Journal Management System

This system is designed for managing research paper submissions.

Researchers can submit papers through the platform. Editors can assign reviewers and manage the review process.

Reviewers can provide feedback and researchers can submit revised versions. The system can keep track of the complete submission history.

---

## 54. Crowdfunding Platform

This website allows people to raise money for different projects or ideas.

---

## 55. Sports League Management System

This system helps manage sports leagues and tournaments.

Teams and players can register for a league. Organizers can create match schedules and update scores.

The system automatically updates standings and player statistics. Fans can also view match information, news, and notifications.

---

## 56. Digital Wallet App

This is a digital wallet application for managing money digitally.

Users can connect accounts or cards and transfer money to other users. The app can also support QR payments and bill payments.

Users can view transaction history, track spending, and set budget alerts. A PIN or biometric-lock simulation can be added for security.

---

## 57. Gaming Tournament Portal

This platform is used to organize gaming tournaments.

Players and teams can register for tournaments. The system can automatically create tournament brackets and update match results.

Users can view live standings, team information, prize details, and tournament updates.

---

## 58. Virtual Stock Trading App

This is a paper-trading application where users can practice stock trading without using real money.

Users receive virtual money and can buy or sell stocks using live or simulated market prices.

The app can show profit and loss, price charts, indicators, and trading history. It can also include tutorials for beginners and trading competitions.

---

## 59. Home Services Booking App

This application helps users find and book home services such as plumbing, electrical work, cleaning, and other services.

Users can search service providers, check ratings and reviews, and choose an available appointment.

After the service is completed, users can confirm the work and upload photos if needed. Service providers can manage their bookings and earnings through their own dashboard.
