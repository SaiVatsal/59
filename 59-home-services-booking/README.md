# Project 59: Home Services Booking App (ProFixer)

A verified home services, contractor matching, appointment scheduling, and escrow payment platform built with **React 18 + Vite 5 + Tailwind CSS 3.4 + Zustand 4.5**.

## Features
- **Service Category Catalog**: Plumbing, Electrical, Deep Cleaning, HVAC Repair, Appliance Diagnostics, and Lawn Care.
- **Verified Contractor Profiles**: Background checks, certified trade licenses, verified ratings & reviews, and hourly rates.
- **Real-Time Appointment Scheduling**: Time slot windows (Morning, Afternoon, Evening), address entry, emergency 24/7 rush dispatch toggle (+25%), and job scope notes.
- **Escrow Hold & In-App Payment**: Transparent pricing breakdown including property protection insurance and escrow hold release upon job sign-off.
- **Customer Booking Management**: Active and historical booking records with status tracking and completion verification.
- **Provider Console**: Contractor route management and weekly payout breakdown.
