# Project 58: Virtual Stock Trading App (QuantApex Terminal)

A Bloomberg terminal-inspired virtual/paper stock trading and market simulator built with **React 18 + Vite 5 + Tailwind CSS 3.4 + Zustand 4.5**.

## Features
- **Real-Time Virtual Paper Portfolio**: Net Liquidation Value ($NAV), Cash balance, Unrealized P&L, Realized Gains, and asset allocation breakdown.
- **Live Stock Watchlist & Screener**: Real-time pricing ticks for major equities (NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, MSFT, GOOGL, SPY).
- **Interactive Price Charting**: Timeframe selectors (1D, 1W, 1M, 1Y, ALL) and technical indicator overlays (SMA 20, EMA 50, RSI 14).
- **Order Execution Ticket**: Instant market, limit, and stop-loss trade execution with automatic margin checking and order audit logging.
- **Position & Holdings Management**: Open position tracking with Mark-to-Market valuations and one-click liquidation.
- **Trader Leaderboard & Academy**: Global competition leaderboards and interactive risk management modules.
